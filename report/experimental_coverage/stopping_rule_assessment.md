# Missingness and stopping assessment

## Findings

- Missing eligible configurations are localized to Covertype and reflect a
  computational stopping rule, not random missingness.
- Preflight-infeasible configurations are separated from missing eligible
  configurations.
- No missing runs were imputed or extrapolated.
- KDD Cup 99's reduced eligible grid and undefined ROC-AUC records are treated
  as metric and feasibility limitations.
